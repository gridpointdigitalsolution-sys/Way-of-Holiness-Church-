First Time Visiting?

Plan Your First Visit

We want your first experience to be everything God intended — comfortable, welcoming, and life-changing. Here's what to expect.

🚗

Getting Here

Two branches across two continents — our headquarters at Akobo, Oyo, Nigeria, and our US branch at 11 Brubar Court, Gwynn, MD, USA. Free parking on premises at both locations. Visitors are warmly received at every gathering.

👋

What to Expect

Arrive a few minutes early and our friendly welcome team will greet you. Services typically run 90–120 minutes and include anointed worship, powerful preaching, and a warm, Spirit-filled atmosphere. Come as you are.

👶

Families & Children

Families are our heartbeat. Children's Church runs during Sunday service for ages 4–12. A clean, safe, and Spirit-filled environment for your little ones to encounter God in a way tailored for them.
