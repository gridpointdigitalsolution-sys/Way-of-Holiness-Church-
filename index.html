Who We Are

Three Decades of
God's Faithfulness

Founded by Rev. Dr. Gideon Afolabi and his wife Pastor Mrs. Oluwatosin Afolabi, The Way of Holiness Church — Mind of Christ Ministry has stood for over 30 years as a covenant family bound by faith and stirred by the fire of the Holy Spirit. From a small gathering of hungry believers, the house has grown into a multi-branch ministry serving Christ across Nigeria and the United States.

Born from one man's burning passion for the Person of Christ and the unfiltered Word of God, this house exists to give back to the public the original Gospel — uncompromised, undiluted, unashamed. Whoever walks through our doors finds room at the table. Saint or seeker. Broken or breakthrough. You belong here.
