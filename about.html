Meet The Founders

Set Apart for This Hour.
Together. By Grace.

Behind every Spirit-led house stands a couple yielded to the call. The Way of Holiness Church was not the product of a strategy session — it was born in the secret place. What you see in our pulpit and our pews is the fruit of two lives long surrendered, long obedient, long faithful. This is their assignment. This is our family.

Senior Pastor & Founder

Rev. Dr. Gideon AfolabiSenior Servant

A man set ablaze for the Person of Christ. For more than two decades, Rev. Dr. Gideon Afolabi has carried one burning mandate — to give back to the public the original, undiluted Word of God. From a small gathering of hungry believers to a multi-branch ministry spanning Nigeria and the United States, his life has been a steady offering of prayer, study, and uncompromised preaching. He is a passionate pastor, a devoted husband, and a father in the faith to a generation rising in the holiness of God.

"For I am not ashamed of the Gospel of Christ..." — Romans 1:16

Pastor Mrs. Oluwatosin AfolabiMother in Zion

A woman of grace, conviction, and quiet strength. Pastor Mrs. Oluwatosin Afolabi has stood faithfully alongside her husband from the founding of this ministry — a covering for the women of the house, a tireless builder of the next generation, and a partner in covenant for every assignment God has handed this family. Her prayers carry the weight of a woman who has walked with God in the secret place. Her life preaches before her words ever do.

"Strength and honor are her clothing..." — Proverbs 31:25

The Journey So Far

Our Story

✦

"This church was not founded out of human ambition — it was born out of a divine mandate placed in the heart of one man. A burning commission to give back to the public the original Word of God, raise a people who walk in true holiness, and plant the Kingdom of God on two continents — and counting."

[Founding Year]

The Founding Vision

In a season of fasting and prayer, Rev. Dr. Gideon Afolabi received a clear, burning mandate from God — to plant a house where holiness is not a burden but a joy, where the Word of God is preached unfiltered, and where every believer discovers their true identity in Christ. The Way of Holiness Church — Mind of Christ Ministry was conceived in that secret place.

[Year]

The First Gathering

Beginning with a small, committed group of believers in Nigeria, the church held its first official service. The atmosphere was thick with the presence of God — salvations, healings, and radical encounters marked the very first gathering. The fire had been lit.

[Year]

Mind of Christ Ministry Launched

Out of a hunger for deeper biblical formation, the "Mind of Christ" teaching ministry was formally launched — a Spirit-led approach to transforming how believers think, decide, and live. Lives began to change rapidly as the Word was preached without compromise.

[Year]

Crossing Continents

God's blessing was unmistakable as the congregation grew. The mandate could not be contained to one city or one country. Branches were planted across Nigeria, and the ministry crossed the Atlantic to plant in the United States — the same Gospel, the same fire, two continents now anchored in the Way of Holiness.

Today

Twenty Years & Counting

Two decades on, the Way of Holiness Church — Mind of Christ Ministry stands as a vibrant, growing family of believers across Nigeria and the United States — a place of encounter, transformation, and purpose. With multiple thriving departments, a hungry congregation, and an expanding global footprint, the best is yet to come. The mandate continues. The fire still burns.

What We Stand On

Our Core Values

01

Holiness

God's call to holiness is not a standard to attain — it is a grace to receive. Holiness is not restriction; it is the fullness of life in God. We pursue it together, in love, by the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

02

Prayer

Prayer is the foundation of everything we do. Not a ritual we observe — a lifestyle we cultivate. We believe in the supernatural power of prayer to shift atmospheres, heal bodies, transform minds, and turn nations.

03

The Word

We are a people of the Book. The Bible is the inerrant, authoritative Word of God — our compass, our foundation, our weapon, our daily bread. Every sermon, every department, every decision is anchored in Scripture.

04

Family

You were never designed to walk alone. God places the solitary in families. We are committed to authentic, Spirit-filled community where every person is known, valued, and given room to grow.

05

Discipleship

The Great Commission is our mandate. We are intentional about making disciples — not just converts. Through mentorship, biblical teaching, and life-on-life investment, we raise believers who change their world.

06

Excellence

God deserves our very best. In worship, in service, in how we treat people — we pursue excellence as an act of worship. Not perfectionism. Wholehearted devotion to His glory.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

📖

The Bible

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God — the supreme and final guide for Christian faith and practice.

✝

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing in three Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — co-equal, co-eternal, and co-existent.

🕊

Salvation

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. Jesus Christ is the only way to the Father and the only Saviour of the world.

🔥

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day ministry of the Holy Spirit — including the baptism of the Holy Spirit, spiritual gifts, and the supernatural power available to every believer.

⬆

The Resurrection

We believe in the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of all the dead, and the bodily return of Christ to establish His eternal Kingdom.

🌍

The Great Commission

Every believer is called to make disciples of all nations — to preach the Gospel with signs and wonders following, until Christ's return.
